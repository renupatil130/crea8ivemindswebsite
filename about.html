Our Story

ONE STUDIO, BUILT TO COVER EVERY ANGLE OF YOUR BRAND.

Crea8ive Minds started with a simple frustration: brands were hiring five different vendors — a designer here, a video editor there, a separate marketing freelancer — and ending up with a brand that didn't feel like one thing.

So we built a studio that does it all in-house: branding, web & app development, video & 3D production, and digital marketing. One team, one vision, one point of contact — from Davangere, for brands anywhere.
