FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to hire separate agencies for design, video and marketing?

No — that's the whole point of Crea8ive Minds. Branding, web/app development, video & 3D, and digital marketing are all handled in-house, so your brand stays consistent across every channel.

Do you work with businesses outside Davangere?

Yes. We work with clients across Karnataka and remotely with brands anywhere — most of our process (design reviews, calls, files) works smoothly online.

What's included in "Online & Offline Marketing Solutions"?

It means your digital campaigns (SEO, social, Meta/Google Ads, WhatsApp marketing) and your offline presence (posters, packaging, shop & event branding, printing) are planned together as one strategy.

How do we get started?

Reach out through the contact page, WhatsApp, or call us directly. We'll set up a quick call to understand your brand and share a plan and quote.
