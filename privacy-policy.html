Home/Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026  ·  Location: Davangere, Karnataka, India

1. Introduction

Welcome to Crea8ive Minds. We are a full-service creative studio based in Davangere, Karnataka, providing branding, graphic design, web and app development, video production, 3D Blender animation, digital marketing, and print & event branding services.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when visiting our website or communicating with our studio.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when making project inquiries or contacting our studio:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, phone number, email address, and business details when filling out our contact form or messaging us via WhatsApp.
  • Project Briefs & Assets: Information regarding your required service, design preferences, brand logos, copy, and project specifications.
  • Technical Usage Data: Non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited to help us optimize website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly used to deliver high-quality creative and digital services:

  • To evaluate your requirements and provide accurate project proposals and quotations.
  • To communicate project milestones, design proofs, development previews, and updates.
  • To execute digital marketing campaigns (SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads) as requested by your business.
  • To respond to your calls, emails, or WhatsApp messages promptly.

4. Form Submissions & Communication Channels

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, your submission is transmitted securely via AJAX. We also provide direct links to WhatsApp and Email (`crea8ivemindsad@gmail.com`). We do not spam your inbox or send unsolicited promotional messages.

5. Client Asset Confidentiality

We treat all client business data, draft concepts, source code, credentials, and custom visual assets with complete confidentiality. Your project assets are used solely for fulfilling your contracted scope of work and are never sold, leased, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

6. Third-Party Integrations

Our website utilizes reputable third-party services including Google Fonts, Google Maps (location embed), and WhatsApp Business API. These platforms operate under their respective privacy policies.
